## What Stage L builds

The Incendiary Boost (canonical F-2): anyone deposits svZCHF or sUSDS one-sided into der Bodensee in exchange for a 14-day supplementary AuMM emission stream directed at a gauged pool of their choice — funded from the existing fixed block emission (F-7 Step 1 priority skim off the LP tranche), never new inflation. Stage H already wired the *consumption* side: the distributor subtracts `IIncendiaryRegistry.integratedSkim` in its continuous-leg accrual per H-D29, defaulting to `address(0)` (zero skim) until governance binds the registry. Stage L ships the *producer* — the registry that sells boosts, prices them, caps them, and reports both the global skim and the per-pool delivery integral.

### L-D1 — Architecture: one contract + one fix-forward — status LOCKED

Stage L ships `src/incendiary/IncendiaryRegistry.sol`, the concrete producer behind the H-D29 forward-dep `IIncendiaryRegistry`. It carries the full registry surface: the purchase entry, the price EMA (L-D5), per-epoch bucket accounting (L-D6 / L-D7 / L-D9), the global `integratedSkim(from,to)` already consumed by the distributor, and a new per-pool `boostIntegral(pool,from,to)` for delivery (L-D8). The only other edit is an additive I13-class fix-forward on the tagged `EmissionDistributor` (per-pool settle/claim reads `boostIntegral`) — its test blast radius is sized at the L pre-flight beat, not at first prompt (the I13 lesson). The Stage D `AureumFeeRoutingHook` Incendiary leg stays dormant (`incendiaryModule` never set), exactly as the governance-deposit leg already does — no Stage D contract is touched.

### L-D2 — Deposit path: donate, no BPT — status LOCKED

Pay tokens are strictly svZCHF or sUSDS — both native der Bodensee constituents, so no swap leg is required. The registry mirrors `AureumGovernance`'s deposit tail (`AureumGovernance.sol:193-199`): pull via `safeTransferFrom(buyer → BODENSEE_CHANNEL)`, then `BODENSEE_CHANNEL.donate(payToken, amount)`. `SwapAndDepositToBodensee.donate` (G-D21) adds one-sided via `AddLiquidityKind.DONATION` and hard-reverts `BptMintedOnDonation` if any BPT would mint — so no BPT exists in this design (a directed epoch-emission is unrelated to any der Bodensee LP position). The registry is added to the channel's `authorizedDonators` allowlist at deploy/wiring. The deposit is non-refundable and 100% to der Bodensee.

### L-D3 — Phase gate: post-Year-1 only — status LOCKED

Purchases revert until `block.number > AureumTime.year1EndBlock(GENESIS_BLOCK)`. H-D29 locked the F-7 Step 1 skim to the distributor's continuous-leg only; a boost sold during the bootstrap or transition phase would route a deposit yet never skim, breaking the H-D26 conservation identity. Gating sales to the continuous phase keeps producer and consumer phase-aligned with zero Stage H change. F-2 / F-3 read as if Incendiary could run earlier; the post-Y1 restriction is an Aureum implementation lock, queued as an aumm-site edit.

### L-D4 — Pricing: 95% entitlement, 5% anti-gaming haircut — status LOCKED

Entitlement `= value_in_AuMM_at_EMA × 95%`, where the AuMM value of the deposit is taken at the EMA rate (L-D5). The 5% is never minted and never collected anywhere (no treasury — the no-creator-fees hard rule) — it simply is not skimmed, so it remains in the distributor's `Remaining` and accrues to the other qualified pools through normal CCB / equal-split. Its sole purpose is anti-gaming friction: a round-trip (dump AuMM → buy boost at the depressed price → buy back) pays the 5% haircut on top of the 60-day-EMA lag, making manipulation unprofitable. Net: the registry's reported skim is 95% of the priced value, and the L-D8 invariant rides that 95% figure.

### L-D5 — Valuation: 60-day price EMA, F-04 maturity gate — status LOCKED

Pricing reads a price EMA the registry maintains itself — the existing `EMASampler` tracks per-pool TVL, not the AuMM/stable rate, so it cannot be reused directly — using `EMASampler`'s constants verbatim: α = 2/61, one sample per `BLOCKS_PER_DAY`, `ema_new = (2·spot + 59·old)/61`, ~21-day half-life / 60-day horizon (`EMASampler.sol:42-47`). Two rails are tracked, AuMM/svZCHF and AuMM/sUSDS, both directly observable from the three-token der Bodensee. The cold-start defense is copied from the whitehat F-04 fix: `EMA_MATURITY_BLOCKS = 60 × BLOCKS_PER_DAY = 432_000`; a rate that has seeded for fewer than 60 days, or never seeded, reverts the purchase (`VotingWeight.sol:30,136-138`). Spot rate is never priced — closing the F-04-class flash-manipulation surface.

### L-D6 — Cap: aggregate 15% of the epoch's emission integral — status LOCKED

The ceiling is on the sum of all boosts active in an epoch, not per pool: Σ allocations in epoch E ≤ 15% × (E's emission integral). Integral form rather than `rate × BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH` because epochs do not divide eras evenly (`BLOCKS_PER_ERA / BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH ≈ 104.29`), so a halving-straddling epoch must be priced across both rates. One shared 15% bucket all pools draw from — the anti-drought guard that keeps at least 85% of every epoch's LP tranche flowing to ordinary LPs.

### L-D7 — Placement: FCFS walk-forward spill — status LOCKED

Boosts fill the shared per-epoch bucket first-come-first-serve. At purchase, starting at the next epoch boundary, the registry walks epochs forward, allocating `min(remaining entitlement, 15%-bucket capacity left)` in each and carrying the remainder forward until exhausted — so a boost spans as many consecutive epochs as the cap requires (a deposit worth 10% of an epoch lands whole; one worth 40% spreads across three). Per-block stream in each epoch = that epoch's allocation / `BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH`. Worked example: boost #1 takes 10% of E; boost #2 takes the remaining 5% in E, then up to 15% in E+1, remainder to E+2; a later buyer gets whatever those leave.

### L-D8 — Delivery: distributor per-pool leg, no new mint channel — status LOCKED

Delivery happens inside the distributor's allocation, honoring F-7 Step 1 / Step 2 ordering: global accrual subtracts `integratedSkim(from,to)` off the LP tranche (wired, H-D29), and the per-pool settle/claim path adds a new leg reading `boostIntegral(pool,from,to)` from the registry over the same interval. The boosted pool's AuMT holders receive the stream through the pool's existing claim machinery; minting stays distributor → `AuMMMinterRouter` (K-D7) — no new mint channel. `boostIntegral` is a new `IIncendiaryRegistry` method, so the L pre-flight beat enumerates inheritors via `grep -rn "is IIncendiaryRegistry"` (G16) before the interface edit. The registry invariant `Σ_pools boostIntegral(p,a,b) = integratedSkim(a,b)` slots into H-D26's `LP_integral + Bodensee_apsum + Incendiary_integral = rate × n` and is unit-tested.

### L-D9 — Accounting: epoch buckets, O(1) skim, additive stacking — status LOCKED

The registry keeps epoch-bucketed cumulative sums so `integratedSkim` and `boostIntegral` stay O(1) regardless of interval size per H-D29, fed by a permissionless `crystallize(from,to)` cache-updater that lives outside the `IIncendiaryRegistry` read surface. The per-(epoch,pool) map is additive: multiple boosts to the same pool in the same epoch stack, subject only to the aggregate L-D6 cap — F-2's literal "one boost per pool per epoch" is dropped (queued aumm-site edit). Full boost provenance is retained via events plus enumerable state, satisfying the "track all boosts acquired" requirement.

### L-D10 — Gauge gate at purchase + mid-boost completion — status LOCKED

Locked at the L1.0 pre-flight. F-2 funds "a gauged pool," so `buyBoost(pool, …)` reverts unless `_gaugeRegistry.isGaugeApproved(pool)` at purchase — the same primitive `EmissionDistributor.recordScore` gates on (step 1). A pool de-gauged mid-boost keeps receiving the already-purchased stream: the entitlement was paid (deposit donated, skim committed) and placed in immutable per-(epoch,pool) buckets, and the `_settlePool` boost leg (L-D14) reads `boostIntegral` without re-checking gauge status. The buyer paid for the full duration, so the stream completes to the pool's LPs regardless of a later revocation — correct, not an exploit (the skim is bounded by the 15% cap and the deposit is non-refundable either way).

### L-D11 — Spot-rate read = direct der Bodensee weighted-pool read — status LOCKED

Locked at L1.0 (Q2 / Q4). der Bodensee is a 40/30/30 WeightedPool (AuMM 40% / sUSDS 30% / svZCHF 30%; `DeployDerBodensee.s.sol`). The registry reads `IVaultExplorer.getPoolData(BODENSEE_POOL).balancesLiveScaled18` + `WeightedPool.getNormalizedWeights()` and computes the weighted-pool spot `rate(AuMM/X) = (bal_X / w_X) / (bal_A / w_A)` for both X = svZCHF and X = sUSDS — both pairs are in the pool, so the sUSDS rail is direct with no 2-hop (Q4). Reusing `TVLOracle.quoteSvZCHF(AUMM, 1e18)` was rejected: it returns 0 for any token absent from the governance-curated `tokenToUnderlying` map (`TVLOracle.sol:290`), and AuMM is never mapped there (the map seeds only stable / pilot tokens). The direct read is self-contained and venue-canonical; it is the per-sample input the L-D5 60-day EMA smooths (raw spot is never priced).

### L-D12 — `boostIntegral` signature — status LOCKED

`boostIntegral(address pool, uint256 from, uint256 to) external view returns (uint256)` — the per-pool delivery counterpart to the existing `integratedSkim(uint256 from, uint256 to)` (`IIncendiaryRegistry.sol:41`), returning the pool's boost allocation over `[from, to]` inclusive in AuMM-wei. Added to `IIncendiaryRegistry` at L1.1; the G16 sweep found the sole consumer is `EmissionDistributor` (+ its four test mock sites), so the interface edit is low-blast.

### L-D13 — `AureumTime` epoch-boundary helpers — status LOCKED

`epochStartBlock(uint256 genesisBlock, uint256 epochIndex)` and `epochEndBlock(uint256 genesisBlock, uint256 epochIndex)` — additive `internal pure` helpers mirroring the existing `month6EndBlock` / `year1EndBlock` boundary family (`AureumTime.sol:62-80`), computing `genesisBlock + epochIndex × BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH` and the inclusive last block. `epochIndex(genesisBlock, blockNumber)` already exists (`AureumTime.sol:48`). The registry's walk-forward placement (L-D7) and the distributor's per-pool boost interval (L-D14) both key on epoch boundaries; named helpers keep the arithmetic testable and consistent. Added at L1.2; no existing `AureumTime` signature is touched.

### L-D18 — L3.1 spot-rate read: runtime index resolution + weighted-pool spot formula — status LOCKED

Locked at the L3.1 pre-flight, closing the L-D17 (L115) divide-direction §12 gate. `_spotRate(address payToken) internal view returns (uint256)` reads the live der Bodensee spot of AuMM denominated in the pay token — the per-sample input the L-D5 60-day EMA smooths. Raw spot is never priced (L-D5); `_spotRate` is internal, with no public view, so it cannot be mistaken for a callable price oracle (the L8 EMA tests assert it through the seed path, where the first sample sets `ema == spot`).

Runtime index resolution is mandatory. der Bodensee registers its three tokens sorted ascending by address (`DeployDerBodensee.s.sol:45-61`), so the index of AuMM / svZCHF / sUSDS in the pool arrays is deploy-address-dependent, not static. `_spotRate` resolves `iA` (AuMM) and `iX` (payToken) by identity-matching `getPoolData(BODENSEE_POOL).tokens[i]` against the `AUMM` immutable and `payToken` — the `TVLOracle._venueRatio` identity-loop pattern (`TVLOracle.sol:247-255`). A hardcoded index would compile and silently misprice. Reverts `TokenNotInPool(token)` if either index is unresolved.

Read shape: one `VAULT_EXPLORER.getPoolData(BODENSEE_POOL)` supplies both `tokens[]` and `balancesLiveScaled18[]` (no separate `getPoolTokens`); one `IWeightedPool(BODENSEE_POOL).getNormalizedWeights()` supplies the index-aligned weights. `balancesLiveScaled18` is the correct leg — the Bodensee stables are `TokenType.WITH_RATE`, so the live-scaled balances carry the rate-provider scaling (the H-D9 Step-1 leg, `TVLOracle.sol:273`).

Formula — Solidity form `spotRate = (bal_X · w_A).divDown(bal_A · w_X)` (`using FixedPoint for uint256`). Since `FixedPoint.divDown(a, b) = a · 1e18 / b` (`FixedPoint.sol:38-44`), the receiver `bal_X · w_A` is NOT pre-multiplied by `1e18` — pre-multiplying would double-scale the result to 1e36 and risk a uint256 overflow at large balances. The math result is therefore `(bal_X · w_A · 1e18) / (bal_A · w_X)`, an 18-decimal fixed-point quantity, where `bal_* = balancesLiveScaled18[i*]` and `w_* = getNormalizedWeights()[i*]` (both 1e18-scaled). Algebraically `(bal_X / w_X) / (bal_A / w_A)` — the weighted-pool spot identity — yielding 18-decimal fixed-point X-per-AuMM (stable wei per 1 AuMM wei). This is stable-per-AuMM per L-D17, so L4 valuation divides the deposit by the EMA of this quantity (`value_in_AuMM = deposit / ema`); `divDown` matches the project convention (`VotingWeight` / `EmissionDistributor` / `CCBShare`). Reverts `ZeroSpotBalance(token)` if `bal_A == 0` or `bal_X == 0` — a silent `divDown` zero would poison the EMA seed. `_spotRate` resolves whichever `payToken` is in the pool; the `{SVZCHF, SUSDS}` whitelist gate stays at L4.1. New imports land at L3.1: `IWeightedPool` (`@balancer-labs/v3-interfaces/contracts/pool-weighted/IWeightedPool.sol`) and `PoolData` (`@balancer-labs/v3-interfaces/contracts/vault/VaultTypes.sol`); two new errors `TokenNotInPool(address)` / `ZeroSpotBalance(address)` — the first L3 use-site errors (the L2.1b deferral lands them here).

### L-D19 — L3.2 price-EMA sampler + maturity gate: EMASampler mirror with explicit rail typing — status LOCKED

Locked at the L3.2 pre-flight. The price EMA is a faithful mirror of the CCB `EMASampler` (`EMASampler.sol:122-154`) — L-D5 binds the constants verbatim — re-keyed from `pool` to `payToken` rail and re-sourced from `_spotRate` (L-D18) instead of `TVL_ORACLE.tvl`. The three EMASampler maps (`tvlEMA` / `lastEMAUpdateBlock` / `emaSeedBlock`) are already collapsed into the L-D17 `RailEMA {ema, lastSampleBlock, seedBlock}` struct, so L3.2 adds behavior, not storage.

`updateRailEMA(address payToken) external returns (uint256 newEMA)` is permissionless — anyone may poke once cadence permits (the `EMASampler` F-D5 posture). Statement order: (1) explicit rail gate — revert `UnknownRail(payToken)` unless `payToken == SVZCHF || payToken == SUSDS`. der Bodensee's `BODENSEE_POOL` is immutable, so `_spotRate` already restricts the valid set to `{sUSDS, svZCHF}`; the explicit gate adds no behavioral safety but makes the rail typed-domain self-documenting and yields a clear revert (chosen over the implicit `TokenNotInPool` fall-through at the L3.2 pre-flight). (2) cadence — revert `TooEarly(block.number, last + BLOCKS_PER_DAY)` when `block.number < rail.lastSampleBlock + AureumTime.BLOCKS_PER_DAY`. (3) `spot = _spotRate(payToken)`. (4) seed-or-smooth: `rail.seedBlock == 0` ⇒ `newEMA = spot` and write `rail.seedBlock = block.number` (L-D17 cold-start sentinel, the F-D15 precedent — avoids the ~60-day cold ramp); else `newEMA = (EMA_ALPHA_NUMERATOR · spot + (EMA_ALPHA_DENOMINATOR - EMA_ALPHA_NUMERATOR) · rail.ema) / EMA_ALPHA_DENOMINATOR`. (5) write `rail.ema = newEMA`, `rail.lastSampleBlock = block.number`, emit `RailEMAUpdated(payToken, spot, newEMA, block.number)`.

Two mechanics are load-bearing and must not drift. The recurrence is plain integer arithmetic, NOT `divDown` — the `/EMA_ALPHA_DENOMINATOR` (= /61) is a true weighted-mean divisor, and both `spot` and `rail.ema` are 18-dec quantities the average preserves; routing it through `FixedPoint.divDown` would multiply by 1e18 and corrupt the scale (the inverse of the L-D18 `_spotRate` case, which DOES need divDown). `(2·spot + 59·ema)` cannot overflow uint256 at any plausible 18-dec rate. The sampler is single-step with no catch-up — the cadence guard enforces only a minimum spacing of `BLOCKS_PER_DAY`; a multi-day gap still applies exactly one smoothing step, and `lastSampleBlock` is set to the actual call block, not `last + BLOCKS_PER_DAY` (OQ-5a-bis, `EMASampler` verbatim). The single-block-spike surface is accepted and defended in depth (the F-04 60-day maturity + the L-D4 5% haircut + the L-D6 15% cap), not at the sampler.

`_maturePrice(address payToken) internal view returns (uint256 ema)` is the mature-gated reader L4.1 `buyBoost` consumes — the F-04 `VotingWeight._positionPower` pattern (`VotingWeight.sol:134-138`) but reverting rather than returning 0: reverts `EMANotMature(payToken, age, EMA_MATURITY_BLOCKS)` when `rail.seedBlock == 0` (age 0, never seeded) or `block.number - rail.seedBlock < EMA_MATURITY_BLOCKS` (the 60-day F-04 gate), else returns `rail.ema`. Internal-only, no public price view — same posture as `_spotRate` at L3.1 (L-D5 "spot never priced"; the EMA is read only through the purchase path). Three errors land at their first use site here — `UnknownRail(address)`, `TooEarly(uint256,uint256)`, `EMANotMature(address,uint256,uint256)` — plus the `RailEMAUpdated(address indexed,uint256,uint256,uint256)` event and an `AureumTime` import (`BLOCKS_PER_DAY`); the L2.1b deferral's "L4/L5" was approximate, L3.2 is genuinely their first emit/revert site. Materialized in two §8e.1s: L3.2a (`updateRailEMA` + `UnknownRail`/`TooEarly` + `RailEMAUpdated` + `AureumTime` import), L3.2b (`_maturePrice` + `EMANotMature`).

### L-D20 — L4.1 buyBoost gates + rate-scaled valuation — status LOCKED

Locked at the L4.1 pre-flight (user decision 2026-06-13), closing a §12 denomination gate the L-D4 / L-D18 shorthand `value_in_AuMM = deposit / ema` glossed. der Bodensee registers both pay tokens as `TokenType.WITH_RATE` with live mainnet rate providers, while AuMM is `STANDARD` / identity (`DeployDerBodensee.s.sol:103-116`), so the L-D18 spot read off `balancesLiveScaled18` bakes the rate in — the EMA is denominated in scaled-18 pay-token per AuMM. A `buyBoost` deposit, by contrast, transfers raw wrapper-token wei via `safeTransferFrom`. Dividing the raw deposit by the rate-scaled EMA under-values it by exactly the wrapper's accrued rate — a systematic, compounding bias (not noise the 60-day EMA averages out) that grows across the continuous phase. The fix: rate-scale the raw deposit to the EMA's scaled-18 basis before pricing.

`_valueInAuMM(address payToken, uint256 amount) internal view returns (uint256 valueInAuMM)` (L4.1a) reads `ema = _maturePrice(payToken)` (L-D19 — reverts `EMANotMature` on unseeded / immature, so the L-D5 maturity gate rides the valuation rather than a separate `buyBoost` statement), then one `VAULT_EXPLORER.getPoolData(BODENSEE_POOL)`, resolves the pay-token index via `_payTokenIndex(data, payToken)` (identity-match on `data.tokens`, reverts `TokenNotInPool` — der Bodensee's indices are address-sorted and not static per L-D18, so the index is resolved at call time, never hardcoded), and scales the deposit: `amountScaled18 = amount.toScaled18ApplyRateRoundDown(data.decimalScalingFactors[iX], data.tokenRates[iX])` — Balancer's canonical raw→scaled-18 conversion `(amount · scalingFactor).mulDown(tokenRate)` (`ScalingHelpers.sol:38-44`), applying both the decimal factor `10^(18-decimals)` and the rate provider's rate, round down (conservative skim — the protocol never over-skims ordinary LPs). Then `valueInAuMM = amountScaled18.divDown(ema)` — scaled-18-X ÷ (scaled-18-X-per-AuMM) = AuMM-wei (18-dec). `toScaled18ApplyRateRoundDown` is decimals-robust, so the path is correct for any pay-token decimals; svZCHF / sUSDS are 18-dec (scalingFactor = 1) so the rate is the active term. New `using ScalingHelpers for uint256` + import `@balancer-labs/v3-solidity-utils/contracts/helpers/ScalingHelpers.sol` — no name collision with the existing `using FixedPoint for uint256` (disjoint function sets). `_payTokenIndex` is a focused single-index helper; the committed `_spotRate` loop (which finds both `iA`/`iX`) is left untouched — no refactor of tested code.

`buyBoost(address pool, address payToken, uint256 amount) external returns (uint256 entitlement)` (L4.1b) gates in order: (1) phase — `if (block.number <= AureumTime.year1EndBlock(GENESIS_BLOCK)) revert BoostsNotOpen(block.number, AureumTime.year1EndBlock(GENESIS_BLOCK))` (L-D3, continuous-phase only); (2) rail — `if (payToken != address(SVZCHF) && payToken != address(SUSDS)) revert UnknownRail(payToken)` (L-D2; reuses the L3.2a `UnknownRail` error — the pay token is the rail, the same {SVZCHF,SUSDS} typed domain, mirroring the L3.2a explicit-gate choice over an implicit fall-through); (3) gauge — `if (!GAUGE_REGISTRY.isGaugeApproved(pool)) revert PoolNotGauged(pool)` (L-D10); (4) amount — `if (amount == 0) revert ZeroAmount()`; (5) maturity — enforced inside `_valueInAuMM` via `_maturePrice`. Then `entitlement = _valueInAuMM(payToken, amount) * (10_000 - HAIRCUT_BPS) / 10_000` — the L-D4 5% anti-gaming haircut as plain-integer basis-points (`value × 9_500 / 10_000`), the codebase BPS convention (`AureumGovernance.sol:294`, `VaultClassRegistry.sol:279`), not FixedPoint `mulDown`; exact, no scale drift.

Sequencing — `buyBoost` is built bottom-up so every intermediate commit is a safe scaffold. L4.1a lands the pure-view valuation internals (`_payTokenIndex` + `_valueInAuMM`); L4.1b lands the gated external `buyBoost` returning `entitlement` as a quote — it reads (gates + valuation) but takes no deposit and places nothing, so no state changes and no funds move. The L-D2 deposit routing (`safeTransferFrom` + `donate`) and the L-D7 FCFS placement call wire into `buyBoost` only after the L5 placement internals exist, as the final end-to-end wiring sub-step — there is never a half-built `buyBoost` that takes a deposit without placing the boost. New errors land at their L4.1b use site: `BoostsNotOpen(uint256 currentBlock, uint256 year1EndBlock)`, `PoolNotGauged(address pool)`, `ZeroAmount()`; `UnknownRail` / `TokenNotInPool` / `EMANotMature` are reused.

### L-D21 — L5.1 epoch emission integral + 15% cap basis — status LOCKED

Locked at the L5.0 pre-flight. The L-D6 aggregate cap is 15% of epoch E's emission integral — the total AuMM emitted across E's blocks. Because BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH (100_800) is far smaller than BLOCKS_PER_ERA (10_512_000), an epoch straddles at most one halving, so the integral is the sum over at most two era sub-intervals of `rate × subLen`. `_epochEmissionIntegral(uint256 epoch) internal view returns (uint256)` mirrors the distributor's `_lpTrancheIntegral` era-split walk (`EmissionDistributor.sol:335-349`, H-D30): from `cursor = epochStartBlock(GENESIS_BLOCK, epoch)`, read `era = AureumTime.eraIndex(GENESIS_BLOCK, cursor)`, `eraEnd = AureumTime.nthHalvingBlock(GENESIS_BLOCK, era + 1) - 1`, clamp `subTo = min(eraEnd, epochEndBlock(GENESIS_BLOCK, epoch))`, snapshot the era-constant `rate = AUMM.blockEmissionRate(cursor)` (`AuMM.sol:127-131`, `GENESIS_RATE >> era`, piecewise-constant within an era), accumulate `rate × (subTo - cursor + 1)`, advance `cursor = subTo + 1`; at most two iterations. `_epochCap(uint256 epoch) internal view returns (uint256)` returns `_epochEmissionIntegral(epoch) × BOOST_CAP_BPS / 10_000` — the plain-integer basis-points form (the L-D20 / `AureumGovernance.sol:294` convention). Both are internal-view; `AUMM.blockEmissionRate` (the immutable `IAuMM AUMM`, L-D16) is the only external call.

### L-D22 — L5.2 FCFS walk-forward placement — status LOCKED

Locked at the L5.0 pre-flight. `_placeBoost(address pool, uint256 entitlement) internal` distributes the L4 entitlement across future epochs per L-D7. Starting at the next epoch boundary `e = AureumTime.epochIndex(GENESIS_BLOCK, block.number) + 1` (the current and all past epochs are never targeted — the immutability the L5.3 view-walk relies on), the function walks forward: `cap = _epochCap(e)` (L-D21), `used = epochSkimAllocated[e]`, `capacity = cap > used ? cap - used : 0`; when `capacity > 0`, `alloc = min(remaining, capacity)`, write `epochSkimAllocated[e] += alloc` (the aggregate 15% bucket) and `epochPoolSkim[e][pool] += alloc` (the additive per-(epoch,pool) delivery map, stacking per L-D9), `remaining -= alloc`, emit `BoostPlaced(pool, e, alloc)`; advance `e++` until `remaining == 0`. Termination holds because far-future epochs are empty (`capacity == cap > 0` while `blockEmissionRate > 0`, i.e. until `era ≥ 256`, ~1000 years), so a non-zero remainder always finds capacity. The loop length is `ceil(entitlement / per-epoch-cap)` — a boost worth K epochs of cap spans K epochs and the buyer pays that gas (L-D7 FCFS accepted, no max-span cap). New event `BoostPlaced(address indexed pool, uint256 indexed epoch, uint256 amount)` — boost provenance per the L-D9 "track all boosts acquired" requirement.

### L-D23 — L5.3 view strategy: direct epoch-walk, crystallize dropped — status LOCKED

Locked at the L5.0 pre-flight (user decision 2026-06-13). L-D9 locked a permissionless `crystallize` prefix-sum cache for O(1) views, and L-D17 deferred its slot shape to L5.3 pending the O(1) algorithm. The access-pattern analysis at the L5.0 pre-flight retired that approach: `boostIntegral` cannot be cheaply O(1) (a per-pool prefix sum `cumPoolSkim[pool][e]` costs O(boosted-pools × epochs) of `crystallize` work and storage, unbounded in pools), `integratedSkim`'s interval is already era-bounded by the distributor's `_lpTrancheIntegral` era-split (≤ BLOCKS_PER_ERA/BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH ≈ 104 epochs, 0—2 typical), and a global-only cache yields O(1) only while `crystallize` is current — which, being permissionless and unincentivised, it may not be. So both views direct-walk the epoch buckets, mirroring the distributor's own cadence-bounded `_lpTrancheIntegral` era-walk posture, and the crystallize cache is DROPPED: L-D9's cache is superseded, L-D17's deferred L5.3 cache slots are cancelled, and Stage L's storage is final as materialized at L2.1b (the three mappings + `RailEMA`, no additions).

Both `integratedSkim(uint256 from, uint256 to)` and `boostIntegral(address pool, uint256 from, uint256 to)` widen the L2.1a stubs from `pure`/`return 0` to `view` and sum, over each epoch `e` in `[epochIndex(from), epochIndex(to)]`, `_epochOverlapBlocks(e, from, to) × (bucket[e] / BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH)` — `bucket = epochSkimAllocated[e]` for the global skim, `epochPoolSkim[e][pool]` for the per-pool leg. The shared `_epochOverlapBlocks(uint256 epoch, uint256 from, uint256 to) internal view returns (uint256)` returns `min(to, epochEnd(e)) − max(from, epochStart(e)) + 1` (the loop range guarantees overlap, so no underflow). `_epochOverlapBlocks` reads the `GENESIS_BLOCK` immutable (hence `view`, not `pure`); `AureumTime.BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH` is the constant divisor.

Per-block delivery is model (i) — the per-block stream is the divide-first fixed quantity `bucket[e] / BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH`, so a partial-epoch integral is `overlapBlocks × perBlock`. This tiles exactly across the distributor's consecutive accrual queries (`integratedSkim(cursor, to)` then `integratedSkim(to+1, …)` sum to the union integral), the decisive property given accrual frequency; model (ii) (multiply-first `overlapBlocks × bucket / BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH`) is exact on full epochs but sheds rounding dust at every query split, accumulating with query frequency, so it is rejected. The model-(i) dust is per-epoch (`bucket[e] mod BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH`, under BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH wei ≈ 1e-13 AuMM, never delivered) — cap-safe and negligible.

Conservation: the L-D8 invariant relaxes from `Σ_pools boostIntegral(p,a,b) = integratedSkim(a,b)` to `Σ_pools boostIntegral(p,a,b) ≤ integratedSkim(a,b)`. Per block the global term is `floor(epochSkimAllocated[e] / BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH)` and the summed per-pool terms are `Σ_p floor(epochPoolSkim[e][p] / BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH) ≤ floor(Σ_p … / BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH)`, so the per-pool floors sum to at most the global floor — the cap-safe direction: the distributor subtracts `integratedSkim` from the LP tranche and the per-pool legs deliver `Σ_pools boostIntegral`, so the gap (sub-unit per pool per block) is simply never minted, never over-minted against the 21M cap (H-D26 preserved). Exact equality is unachievable with floored integer division over a pool set the registry cannot enumerate; the `≤` is a forced, documented consequence, not a chosen relaxation.
