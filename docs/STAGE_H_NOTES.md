### H-D1 — Oracles in-stage as H2a (concrete TVLOracle) + H2b (concrete EfficiencyOracle) — status LOCKED

Concrete oracle implementations arrive as sibling sub-step families inside Stage H: H2a delivers the production-ready `ITVLOracle` implementation; H2b delivers the deterministic on-chain successor to Mock efficiency feeds under `IEfficiencyOracle`. The team rejected a separate pre-mainnet micro-stage devoted solely to oracles because Stage G proved that a single long-lived branch with internal H2a/H2b rows keeps tagging, completion logs, and branch hygiene contiguous without sacrificing scope boundaries. Interfaces are already stabilized—`ITVLOracle` at Stage F milestone F0.2 per F-D11 and FINDINGS-linked G-D25c; `IEfficiencyOracle` at G-D23(ii) with the same FINDINGS scaffolding—plus Mock variants under `test/fork/mocks/`, so substantive remaining work sits in auditable Solidity rather than in interface churn. Embedding that work alongside the emission distributor aligns dependency ordering with GaugeRegistry callers that already anticipate LIVE feeds at Stage H handoff rather than dangling cross-repo promises. Operational risk concentrates in deployment sequencing reviewed once at H2 instead of juggling an extra stage-complete tag prematurely. Canonical TVL-shape discussion remains tracked via FINDINGS OQ-22, while sibling-oracle efficiency semantics tied to GaugeEligibility snapshots remain FINDINGS OQ-G1 complements to G-D23. Primary anchors: F-D11, G-D23, G-D25c, FINDINGS OQ-22, FINDINGS OQ-G1.

### H-D2 — Separate BodenseeBootstrapChannel contract (not inline in distributor) — status LOCKED

F-0 bootstrap emissions funnel through `src/emission/BodenseeBootstrapChannel.sol`, a narrowly scoped ledger that accrues per block toward AuMM earmarked for der Bodensee and exposes a deterministic deposit-and-distribute path marrying one-sided AuMM placements with der Bodensee liquidity while respecting the ancillary F-2 swap-and-buyback machinery. Folding those mechanics into the post-Month-10 emission distributor would interleave unrelated bootstrap accounting with accumulator math meant for gauged pools, increasing failure modes around reserve deltas and treasury routing. Separation mirrors Stage G carve-outs: SwapAndDepositToBodensee stayed outside GaugeRegistry per G-D11, and reserve-delta enforcement remained isolated inside the transient-storage primitive per G-D21. Dedicated contracts keep auditors focused on discrete trust boundaries touching der Bodensee’s bilateral reserves rather than a monolith that evolves every schedule tweak. Operational deploy scripts can instantiate the channel independently of downstream schedule logic, simplifying smoke tests tied solely to Bodensee accounting. Lifecycle clarity—bootstrap rail retires cleanly after Month 10 logic hands off weights to the distributor—reduces regressions tracked through formal reviews. Anchors tying back to rationales: F-0, F-2, G-D11, G-D21.

### H-D3 — F-7 share weights = step function sampled at BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH boundaries — status LOCKED

F-7’s CCB-weighted pool shares recomputed from live score inputs update only once per BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH boundary rather than recomputing on every Ethereum block cited by the textual “every block” reading in `11_formulas.md` F-7. Literal every-block fidelity would amplify gas by forcing synchronized reads across every gauged pool’s TVL lanes and multiplier state on arbitrarily dense swap traffic, collapsing feasibility on mainnet. Instead the refresh cadence aligns with Stage F precedent that pins multiplier evolution to epoch granularity via F-D24, keeping F-8 style multiplier math and F-7 share math co-tenants on BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH (OQ-3 anchors the 100_800 block cadence practitioners already rely on elsewhere). Harmonizing timelines reduces divergence bugs where auditors must reconcile contradictory cadences between boost updates and emitted weights feeding claim math. Divergence from verbatim spec wording stays documented herein until a FINDINGS row records the discrepancy if downstream surfaces ever assume block-perfect weight streams. FINDINGS elevation is warranted if UI dashboards, aggregator heuristics, or governance payloads treat block-frequency F-7 as authoritative; schedule that decision no later than H4 once integration tests illuminate consumer expectations. Anchors referenced during lock: F-7, F-8, F-D24, OQ-3.

### H-D4 — Pull / lazy accrual via MasterChef-style accRewardPerScoreUnit accumulator — status LOCKED

The distributor advances a solitary global `accRewardPerScoreUnit`, FixedPoint-encoded with 18-decimal normalization, ticking forward whenever an emission-bearing block segments accrue protocol mints into the staking ledger. Participant claims dereference snapshots of prior `accRewardPerScoreUnit`, multiplying deltas by persisted pool-specific score totals to reconcile AuMM payouts without iterating every deposit on payout. Matching spec language—“deposit triggers accrual; withdrawal stops it immediately”—means deposit and withdraw mutations refresh per-stake bookkeeping exactly like canonical MasterChef pull semantics instead of spraying push transfers each block across every vault. Attempting naive push accruals would collide with Gas realities once hundreds of gauges exist and would undermine the intra-epoch constancy ensured by H-D3’s stepwise weights. Accumulator bookkeeping must obey F12 and F13 signed-delta safeguards because CCB-derived scores shrink as aggressively as they grow epoch to epoch under tournament losses. Neglecting int/uint discipline while folding negative corrections risks underflow faults identical to Stage F counterexamples that forced explicit signed-add middleware. Operational monitoring therefore treats both boosts and slashes as first-class events feeding the accumulator rather than ephemeral hints. Supporting references: F-7, H-D3, F12, F13.

### H-D5 — Pool-scoped distributor; no IGaugeRegistry enumeration; incremental totalScore aggregate — status LOCKED

Each mutating distributor entry accepts an explicit pool key and consults `IGaugeRegistry.isGaugeApproved(pool)` before mutating aggregates, guaranteeing no hidden enumeration across the upstream registry ABI. GaugeRegistry consciously omits iterators per G-D5 and G-D7, so cloning parallel mirrors inside the emission module would bifurcate truth about which factories remain approved. Instead totalScore evolves via deltas applied alongside every sanctioned score-update path, amortizing bookkeeping to O(1) increments instead of Ω(N) sweeps before claims. Sweep-based reconciliation would resurrect the G16-style mock-registry sweep debt found when interface expansions raced ahead of enumerated consumers in Stage G forks. Liquidity bots and staking front-ends naturally know which pool they target, shifting discovery burdens where context already exists off-chain rather than forcing protocol-level listing APIs. Anchors guarding this rationale: G-D5, G-D7, G16.

### H-D6 — F-1 share denominator (fixed 1/28 vs dynamic 1/N_miliarium) — status LOCKED

H-D6 locks the F-1 equal-split denominator at the literal fixed fraction `1/28` recorded in `11_formulas.md` F-1 and echoed in `04_tokenomics.md` §ix—twenty-eight nominal slots stay constant for the entire Months 0—10 window no matter how many Miliarium pools are live on-chain. For any live count `N < 28`, each block leaves `(28 − N)/28` of `lp_share × block_emission` structurally unminted—never parked in a treasury slot, never deferred for catch-up minting—so the withheld tranche is permanently uncreatable by construction rather than stored for later release. The posture continues Stage C’s Bitcoin-philosophy MAX_SUPPLY = 21M AuMM hard-cap continuity: early windows that trail the notional schedule issue less than the formula’s ceiling allows, compressing effective lifetime supply under the asymptotic cap with no clawback or refill path. Months 11—12 inherit the same literal automatically because F-3’s blend uses `(1 - α(block)) × (1/28)` on the equal-split leg while α linearly ramps, so no separate denominator decision is needed for F-3 once this lock is fixed. Attempting dynamic `N` from `IMiliariumRegistry` enumeration would reintroduce the Ω(N) sweeps H-D5 explicitly forbids and would scramble historical allocations whenever pools arrive or depart between epochs on the BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH cadence referenced from OQ-3. FINDINGS requires no new OQ while implementation matches the canonical `1/28` wording; only a deliberate future departure from `11_formulas.md` F-1 would force a coordinated `aumm-site` §ix amendment plus a numbered open-question row. Anchors closing the record: F-1 as canonical share rule, F-3 as cascade beneficiary, H-D5 as the anti-enumeration guard, MAX_SUPPLY as the Stage C issuance ceiling, `aumm-site` §ix tokenomics as the prose twin, OQ-3 tying epoch discipline to the BLOCKS_PER_EPOCH cadence.

### H-D9 — TVLOracle per-token underlying identification + cross-asset leg arithmetic — status LOCKED

H-D9 instantiates the OQ-22 valuation pipeline as concrete arithmetic for `TVLOracle.tvl(pool)`, locking the sub-decisions left open after OQ-22's pipeline-shape pin. Step 1 (per-token RP-aware unwrap) collapses to a single read of `IVaultExplorer.getPoolData(pool).balancesLiveScaled18[i]` — Balancer V3 natively folds `RP.getRate() × balance × decimalScalingFactor` into that field per `VaultTypes.PoolData` semantics, so the oracle inherits the upstream rate-provider accounting without re-deriving it. Per-token "underlying" identification — needed to match tokens to constellation venues — uses a governance-curated `tokenToUnderlying[address]` map owned by `TVLOracle` itself, seeded at construction with known mappings (svZCHF → ZCHF, sUSDS → USDS, waEthUSDC → USDC, st-EURA → EURA, etc.) and extended via the same `setGovernanceContract`-pivoting `governance` slot already locked in H-D8 (no second governance surface); STANDARD tokens default to a self-map (`tokenToUnderlying[token] = token`) seeded explicitly at construction time; ERC-4626 `asset()` introspection is rejected as a runtime fallback because non-ERC4626 rate providers (cbBTC, future bespoke wrappers) break the assumption — the governance map is OQ-8's `BTC_WRAPPERS` precedent applied one level up, prioritizing predictable audit surface over reflective convenience. Step 2 (underlying-to-svZCHF cross-asset leg) uses balance-ratio averaging at constellation venues — for a constellation pool holding both wrapper-of-U and wrapper-of-svZCHF, the implied U→svZCHF rate equals `balanceSvZCHFLeg18 / balanceULeg18` drawn from the same `PoolData` read, with no pool-type-specific curve math (StableMath Jacobian, weighted ratios) consumed; the contribution-side average is taken across a precomputed reverse-map `underlyingToPools[U]` rebuilt on each `addConstellationPool` call from the H-D8 roster plus the `tokenToUnderlying` state. Accuracy posture: the balance-ratio approximation is exact at AMM parity and degrades smoothly off-parity bounded by arbitrage equilibrium and the constellation-roster averaging, with the downstream daily-cadence EMA per OQ-5a-bis absorbing per-block sampling noise — this is a faithful-but-coarse instantiation of OQ-22's "constellation-spot averaging" prose, matching OQ-8's "averaging dilutes manipulation" framing without requiring per-pool Jacobian math, and is the accepted error budget for Stage H; curve-derived spot price (per-pool-type math) remains an optional post-Stage-H precision tightening reserved for the case where mainnet telemetry shows materially wrong F-7 rankings. Phase boundary: Stage H ships Phase 1 = direct venues only — tokens whose underlying lacks a direct constellation venue with svZCHF resolve to `tvl_contribution = 0` non-revert per H-D8 bootstrap semantics; OQ-22 step 2 prose at `docs/FINDINGS.md` L1115 explicitly allows the 2-hop fallback through ZCHF or sUSDS, but that path is deferred to a future H-x micro-stage or Stage K+ and is called out here as a NOTES carry-forward to prevent auditors from inferring 2-hop is live at Stage H ship. svZCHF dual-role (numéraire AND a WITH_RATE token whose `RP.getRate()` returns ZCHF-per-share rather than 1e18-identity) resolves cleanly under the same arithmetic — step 1 unwraps the svZCHF token contribution to ZCHF-equivalent, step 2 converts ZCHF → svZCHF via the same constellation balance-ratio against svZCHF venues, and the round-trip is mathematically self-consistent. Anchors closing the record: OQ-22 step 1 + step 2 (pipeline shape; FINDINGS L1110–L1116, with L1115 the explicit 2-hop carry-forward anchor), OQ-8 (`BTC_WRAPPERS` governance-extensible precedent; FINDINGS L661–L662, L703), H-D8 (constellation roster + governance gate), F-D9 (Miliarium registry placeholder semantics), OQ-5a-bis (daily EMA cadence absorbing per-block α-approximation noise), Balancer V3 `VaultTypes.PoolData` (`balancesLiveScaled18` semantics), `IVaultExplorer.getPoolData` (single read-only entry point for Step 1), Stage K+ (2-hop path future scope).

### H-D10 — EfficiencyOracle intra-epoch accumulation model + EpochEntry ring buffer — status LOCKED

EfficiencyOracle implements `IEfficiencyOracle.efficiencyInputs(pool)` via intra-epoch accumulation with epoch-stamped ring-buffer history — a concrete Phase 1 instantiation of OQ-G1's 3-epoch SMA formula `(swap_fee_revenue_i + yield_fee_revenue_i) / emissions_received_i`. svZCHF numéraire (H-D10 v2 amendment, H1.9-bis): G-D23(i) requires `numeratorSma` and `denominatorSma` to be expressed in the same unit — Phase 1 denominator-unit lock is svZCHF scaled18 for both legs, achieved by routing all push calls through `ITVLOracle.quoteSvZCHF(token, amountScaled18)`, the external wrapper on `TVLOracle._constellationRatio` committed at H2a.10a. Two authorized push entry points accumulate real-time per-pool data within the current epoch: `recordFees(pool, token, amountScaled18)` gated to a governance-settable `feeRecorder` slot (initially the Stage H governance Safe; wired to the fee routing hook or a fee aggregator in a future upgrade) — converts `amountScaled18` of `token` to svZCHF via `tvlOracle.quoteSvZCHF(token, amountScaled18)` then accumulates the svZCHF equivalent to `_accNumerator[pool]`; tokens unmapped in TVLOracle (`quoteSvZCHF` returns 0) are silently skipped without revert, consistent with TVLOracle's skip-on-zero semantics — and `recordEmissions(pool, aummAmountScaled18)` gated to a governance-settable `emissionsRecorder` slot (wired to `EmissionDistributor` at H4 deploy) — converts AuMM emissions to svZCHF via `tvlOracle.quoteSvZCHF(AuMM, aummAmountScaled18)` then accumulates the svZCHF equivalent to `_accDenominator[pool]`. Two constructor immutables support the quoteSvZCHF calls: `ITVLOracle tvlOracle` (the TVLOracle deployed at H2a, stored as an immutable) and `address AuMM` (the AuMM token address, stored as an immutable, used as the `token` argument to `quoteSvZCHF` in `recordEmissions`). Deploy governance prerequisite: `TVLOracle.tokenToUnderlying[AuMM]` must be seeded at deploy with a constellation venue that prices AuMM in svZCHF; if absent, all `recordEmissions` calls produce `denominatorSma = 0` post-warmup, triggering `EfficiencyDataUnavailable(pool)` reverts in GaugeEligibility — governance rescue is to seed the mapping before the first tournament epoch or call `setEmissionsRecorder(address(0))` to disable stale accumulation. Both entry points trigger `_ensureCurrentEpoch(pool)` before accumulating — if `AureumTime.epochIndex(GENESIS_BLOCK, block.number)` has advanced past `_accEpoch[pool]`, the function writes the accumulated pair `(_accNumerator[pool], _accDenominator[pool])` into the history ring at slot `_accEpoch[pool] % 3` with an `EpochEntry { epoch, numerator, denominator }` struct (epoch stamp enables stale-slot validation in the view function), then resets the accumulators to zero and advances `_accEpoch[pool]` to `currentEpoch`. The ring buffer per pool is `EpochEntry[3]` — size 3 matches `SMOOTHING_EPOCHS = 3` per OQ-4 / FINDINGS L501; epoch `e` writes to slot `e % 3`, overwriting data three epochs prior per ring semantics. Stale-slot detection in `_history[pool][slot]` relies solely on the stored `epoch` field — no zero-fill loop on epoch gaps — so `_ensureCurrentEpoch` is O(1) regardless of dormancy duration. `efficiencyInputs(pool)` is a pure `view` function: for each of the three target epochs (currentEpoch − 1, currentEpoch − 2, currentEpoch − 3), it first checks whether `_accEpoch[pool]` matches the target epoch (in which case the in-progress accumulators supply the value, handling the common case where the epoch boundary has not yet triggered `_ensureCurrentEpoch`); it then falls back to the ring slot with epoch-stamp validation; epochs with no record contribute 0 to both numerator and denominator sums; final returns are `sumNumerator / 3` and `sumDenominator / 3` — always dividing by 3, not by the count of valid epochs, so zeros correctly attenuate dormant-pool SMAs toward zero without inflating the per-epoch average. Early-epoch underflow guard: if `currentEpoch < 3`, `efficiencyInputs` returns `(0, 0)` immediately — GaugeEligibility's warmup check per G-D23(v) (`newEpoch − firstTournamentEpoch[pool] < SMOOTHING_EPOCHS`) skips pools before they accumulate 3 epochs of data regardless. Governance pattern mirrors TVLOracle per H-D8: mutable `governance` slot seeded to the Stage A–K Authorizer Safe at construction, pivotable via `setGovernanceContract(address)` at Stage K; `setFeeRecorder(address)` and `setEmissionsRecorder(address)` both `onlyGovernance`, both accepting zero address to disable the respective recorder slot (a safety valve that blocks stale-fee or stale-emissions accumulation after a feed is deprecated). `GENESIS_BLOCK` immutable: constructor parameter, required for `AureumTime.epochIndex` calls; same precedent as `AuMM.sol`. Known Phase 1 limitation: pools dormant for > 3 epochs present `denominatorSma = 0` to GaugeEligibility post-warmup, causing `EfficiencyDataUnavailable(pool)` reverts; governance rescue path is to call `recordEmissions` as the `emissionsRecorder` or reassign the `emissionsRecorder` slot; no automated resolution; deferred to post-Stage-H if mainnet telemetry reveals material impact. Anchors: OQ-G1 (efficiency metric + SMA shape), FINDINGS L501 (`SMOOTHING_EPOCHS = 3`), G-D23(i) (same-unit requirement for numeratorSma + denominatorSma), G-D23(v) (cold-start warmup, `firstTournamentEpoch` in GaugeEligibility), H-D8 (governance pattern mirror), H2a.10a (`TVLOracle.quoteSvZCHF` svZCHF conversion primitive), `AuMM.sol` `GENESIS_BLOCK` constructor pattern, `AureumTime.epochIndex`.

### H-D11 — BodenseeBootstrapChannel mint-at-distribute + permissionless accrue accumulator + AP piecewise integration — status LOCKED

`BodenseeBootstrapChannel` separates F-0 bootstrap mechanics into two semantically distinct surfaces — a permissionless `accrue()` tick that performs pure bookkeeping and a governance-gated `distribute()` that performs the actual `IAuMM.mint` plus der Bodensee one-sided DONATION. Mint timing locks to `distribute()`: `accrue()` advances `lastAccrualBlock` and bumps a `pendingAccrual` accumulator (denominated in AuMM scaled18 units) without ever invoking `IAuMM.mint`, so anonymous callers can permissionlessly drive the accumulator forward without delegating mint authority and without burning mint capacity on spam. Per-block contribution math integrates the piecewise-linear F-0 share — Bootstrap A spans `[genesisBlock, month6EndBlock]` with `bodensee_share(b) = 0.80 − 0.30 × (b − genesisBlock) / (month6EndBlock − genesisBlock)` (linear 80% → 50%); Bootstrap B spans `(month6EndBlock, month10EndBlock]` with `bodensee_share(b) = 0.50 − 0.50 × (b − month6EndBlock) / (month10EndBlock − month6EndBlock)` (linear 50% → 0%). The per-block emission rate `IAuMM.blockEmissionRate(b)` is snapshot once per `accrue()` call rather than re-read per block — Era 0 fully covers the F-0 bootstrap window since Month 10 end ≈ 2.19M blocks and the first halving lands at 10_512_000 blocks per `BLOCKS_PER_ERA` per OQ-3, so the per-call snapshot is exact within bootstrap while the snapshot pattern keeps the contract era-aware for future revisions. Sum across `[lastAccrualBlock + 1, to]` reduces to an arithmetic progression on each bootstrap leg: `Σ bodensee_share(b) × rate = ((first + last) / 2) × n × rate` where `first = bodensee_share(lastAccrualBlock + 1)`, `last = bodensee_share(to)`, `n = to − lastAccrualBlock`, executing in O(1) regardless of interval length. Cross-boundary `accrue()` calls — intervals spanning `month6EndBlock` — split into two AP sums, one ending at `month6EndBlock` under Bootstrap A coefficients and one starting at `month6EndBlock + 1` under Bootstrap B coefficients, preserving exact piecewise semantics without per-block iteration. `pendingAccrual` and `lastAccrualBlock` are the only mutating state surfaces in `accrue()`; `totalDistributed` mutates only in `distribute()`. Anchors closing the record: F-0 (piecewise bootstrap formula), `IAuMM.blockEmissionRate` (Stage C era halving — snapshotted, not reimplemented), `AureumTime.month6EndBlock` and `AureumTime.month10EndBlock` (Stage C boundary helpers), OQ-3 (`BLOCKS_PER_ERA = 10_512_000` — bootstrap window entirely within Era 0), STAGE_H_NOTES.md L126 (Produced interface boundary — permissionless `accrue` + governance `distribute`).

### H-D12 — BodenseeBootstrapChannel one-sided AuMM DONATION via Vault.unlock — mirrors G-D11 / G-D21 transient-storage + reserve-delta discipline; does NOT reuse SwapAndDepositToBodensee.donate — status LOCKED

`distribute()` executes the AuMM-pay-token one-sided injection into der Bodensee through Balancer V3's `IVault.unlock` callback pattern, mirroring `SwapAndDepositToBodensee`'s G-D11 / G-D21 audit-isolation discipline structurally but keying entirely on AuMM as the pay token — `SwapAndDepositToBodensee.donate(IERC20 payToken, uint256 amount)` rejects any `payToken` other than `_svZchf` or `_sUsds` per `src/gauge/SwapAndDepositToBodensee.sol` L329 + L362–L364 explicit `InvalidPayToken` revert, so the bootstrap channel cannot reuse that helper for AuMM. The distribute flow inside the channel: (1) compute `amount = pendingAccrual`; (2) revert if `amount == 0`; (3) zero `pendingAccrual` and add `amount` to `totalDistributed`; (4) `IAuMM.mint(address(this), amount)` (channel holds mint authority via H-D7-pending wiring); (5) snapshot pre-reserve via `_currentReserve(_aummIndex)` mirroring G-D12 reserve-delta discipline; (6) write transient slots `_EXECUTING = true`, `_PENDING_AMOUNT = amount`, `_ORIGINAL_CALLER = msg.sender`; (7) `_vault.unlock(abi.encodeCall(this._distributeCallback, (amount)))`; inside the callback (`OnlyVault` gate + `CallbackPayloadMismatch` revert on payload mismatch) — (8) `IERC20(address(AuMM)).safeTransfer(address(_vault), amount)` then `_vault.settle(IERC20(address(AuMM)), amount)`; (9) build `maxAmountsIn[_aummIndex] = amount` and call `_vault.addLiquidity({ pool: _bodensee, to: address(this), maxAmountsIn, minBptAmountOut: 0, kind: AddLiquidityKind.DONATION, userData: "" })`; (10) post-unlock clear all three transient slots and assert `IERC20(address(AuMM)).balanceOf(address(this)) == 0` via `HelperBalanceNonZero(residual)` revert. Under Balancer V3 `AddLiquidityKind.DONATION` semantics the BPT conferred to `address(this)` is structurally burnt by the pool (DONATION is the "give without receiving" path), so the channel never holds Bodensee BPT post-call — the post-residual check therefore covers AuMM balance only. `_aummIndex` is resolved at construction by introspecting `_vault.getPoolTokens(_bodensee)` for the AuMM token address and stored as an immutable `uint8` to keep callback gas tight; an `IndexLookupFailed` revert fires at construction if AuMM is not found in der Bodensee's token roster, since a misconfigured deploy is unrecoverable. The transient-storage slot layout uses three immutable slot constants (`_EXECUTING_SLOT`, `_PENDING_AMOUNT_SLOT`, `_ORIGINAL_CALLER_SLOT`) derived via `StorageSlotExtension.calculateSlot` per G-D11 — `_PENDING_PAY_TOKEN_SLOT` is unnecessary versus `SwapAndDepositToBodensee` since pay-token is always AuMM (an immutable rather than a runtime parameter). `_distributeCallback` is `external` for `abi.encodeCall` reachability with the same `OnlyVault` + `CallbackPayloadMismatch` revert pattern as G-D11. The audit boundary stays single-purpose: the channel routes AuMM and only AuMM; svZCHF / sUSDS routing through der Bodensee continues to flow through `SwapAndDepositToBodensee` (G-D12 swap fees, G-D21 governance bonds) — explicit non-coupling avoids auditor inference of shared validation. Anchors closing the record: F-0 (bootstrap destination = der Bodensee), G-D11 (audit-isolation precedent), G-D12 (nine-step SwapAndDeposit pattern as structural template), G-D21 (transient-storage + reserve-delta discipline), `IVault.unlock` + `AddLiquidityKind.DONATION` (Balancer V3 callback + add-liquidity kind), `src/gauge/SwapAndDepositToBodensee.sol` L329 + L362–L364 (explicit AuMM rejection — non-reusable for bootstrap), H-D7 (mint authority handoff timing — OPEN, resolves at H10).

### H-D13 — BodenseeBootstrapChannel lifecycle clamp: to = min(block.number, month10EndBlock) + empty-interval short-circuit + no selfdestruct — status LOCKED

`accrue()` applies a lifecycle clamp — `to = min(block.number, month10EndBlock)` — before computing the AP sum, so calls after the bootstrap window closes still advance `lastAccrualBlock` to `month10EndBlock` (capturing any residual emission) but no longer accumulate further. The empty-interval guard `if (from > to) return;` (where `from = lastAccrualBlock + 1`) short-circuits immediately when `lastAccrualBlock` has already been advanced to `month10EndBlock`, avoiding the AP formula on a degenerate zero-width interval — an inert but harmless no-op otherwise avoided for clarity. `BodenseeBootstrapChannel` has no `selfdestruct`, no `disable`, no `pause` — EIP-6049 / EIP-4758 make `selfdestruct` semantically deprecated on mainnet post-Cancun; the contract becomes a permanent, zero-activity fixture after the bootstrap window closes, with `pendingAccrual` and `totalDistributed` readable on-chain as historical anchors. Governance authority over `distribute()` remains live post-window so any residual `pendingAccrual` accumulated at the boundary tick can still be flushed — the governance slot pivots to the Stage K on-chain governance contract at H-D14's handoff path. Anchors closing the record: F-0 (bootstrap window definition — Month 0 through Month 10), `AureumTime.month10EndBlock` (Stage C boundary helper — the lifecycle clamp upper bound), EIP-6049 / EIP-4758 (selfdestruct semantic deprecation on mainnet post-Cancun), H-D14 (governance-only `distribute()` + Stage K slot handoff).

### H-D14 — BodenseeBootstrapChannel governance-only distribute() + immutable BODENSEE_POOL recipient — stronger than H-D8 / H-D10 mutable slots; F-2 not coupled in-channel — status LOCKED

`distribute()` is gated by an `onlyGovernance` modifier matching the H-D8 / H-D10 governance-slot pattern — the governance slot is mutable via `setGovernanceContract(address)` (rejecting `address(0)` per TVLOracle precedent) so the Stage A–K Authorizer Safe seeded at construction pivots to the Stage K on-chain governance contract at handoff. The recipient pool — der Bodensee — is bound at construction as an immutable `BODENSEE_POOL` rather than a mutable slot: there is no `setBodenseePool` setter, and the `_aummIndex` introspection at construction per H-D12 fixes the AuMM token position in the Bodensee roster permanently. This is structurally stronger than the H-D8 TVLOracle roster (mutable additions/removals via `addPool` / `removePool`) and the H-D10 EfficiencyOracle recorder slots (mutable to `address(0)` for deprecation) — F-0 names der Bodensee specifically as the bootstrap destination, so any future pivot would not be a configuration change but a complete redeployment with a new bootstrap-aware contract. The decision to make `distribute()` governance-gated rather than permissionless or block-cadence-driven follows from H-D11's mint-at-distribute lock: mint authority is the load-bearing scarce resource, and a permissionless distribute would let any caller force-flush `pendingAccrual` at an arbitrary block, donating to der Bodensee at the caller's chosen swap-price window — governance gating gives the protocol explicit timing control over the F-0 → F-1 boundary tick and over any residual flush post-Month-10. F-2 (Stage L Incendiary fee burn) is not coupled in this channel — der Bodensee swap fees and yield fees continue to route through `AureumProtocolFeeController` per Stage D, and the channel never reads or writes fee-router state; the audit boundary stays AuMM-mint-and-donate only. Anchors closing the record: H-D8 (TVLOracle mutable governance slot precedent), H-D10 (EfficiencyOracle mutable recorder slot precedent), H-D11 (mint-at-distribute justifying governance gating), H-D12 (immutable `_aummIndex` introspection at construction), F-0 (der Bodensee as bootstrap destination — pool identity locked at spec level), Stage K (governance handoff path), Stage D `AureumProtocolFeeController` (F-2 routing — not coupled here), STAGE_H_NOTES.md L126 (Produced interface boundary).

## Architecture

Stage H concentrates new Solidity under `src/emission/`—the emission distributor itself, concrete `TVLOracle` at H2a, concrete `EfficiencyOracle` at H2b, the separate BodenseeBootstrapChannel per H-D2, and the Stage L forward-dependency stub `src/incendiary/IIncendiaryRegistry.sol` slated for H7. AuMM enforces era halving internally through `IAuMM.blockEmissionRate(blockNumber)` returning `GENESIS_RATE >> eraIndex`, which means Stage H consumes (does not reimplement) the halving schedule—and every halving-aware call site in Stage H collapses into a single `IAuMM.blockEmissionRate(block.number)` read. Routine orchestration traverses four schedule phases tied to formulae references: F-0 piecewise bootstrap across Months 0—6 followed by Months 6—10, F-1 equal splitting across twenty-eight nominal Miliarium lanes through Month 10, F-3 linear blending from equal weights toward CCB-weighted shares spanning Months 11—12, and F-7’s full tournament allocations once Year 2 begins. Readers reach backward into Stage F and Stage G solely through immutable, read-only accessors—TVL EMA probes, multiplier reads, roster membership predicates, gauge approval—with mutating workload isolated to accumulator slots inside Stage H contracts plus mediated `IAuMM.mint` calls once deployments satisfy the eventual H-D7 `setMinter` timing gate. Matching H-D5, every guarded entry exposes an explicit `address pool` parameter and predicates on `IGaugeRegistry.isGaugeApproved(pool)`, so enumeration across the upstream registry ABI never enters the distributor design.

## Interface boundary

Stage G G-DP4’s forward-dependency convention is the precedent: unreachable governance slots stayed explicit placeholders until successors landed, and Stage H repeats that posture for stubs it does not own yet.
