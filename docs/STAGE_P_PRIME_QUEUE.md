**Source of truth, and what this file deliberately does NOT carry.** `STAGE_P_PRIME_PLAN.md` §"Collapsed defect list" (L60—L168) is the authority for `RC`, `audit files`, `post-review severity`, `fix intent` and `ordering constraints`; those five columns are transcribed from it and it wins on any disagreement. The root-cause PROSE is not restated here — a reader who needs why a row exists reads the plan's table, so there is one description of each defect in the repository rather than two that drift.
**`redeploy unit` — the closed seven-token vocabulary (PP-D40 (ii), (iii)).** `hook`, `ccb`, `emission-stack`, `tvl-oracle`, `governance`, `token-schedule`, `source-only`. Exactly one per row, naming the ENTRY contract the fix edits and never PP-D12's transitive closure, which PP4.0 computes from the immutables. `token-schedule` MEANS a chain restart: it takes `AuMM` with it and abandons `GENESIS_BLOCK`.
**`done-criteria` — an artifact, never prose (PP-D40 (vi)).** The form is `path::case`, so PP4.52 reads a green run rather than a judgment. Names at creation are the INTENDED artifact and are binding on PP3.2, which either creates it or records why the row cannot reproduce.
**Unfilled cells read `—` rather than blank,** so a cell awaiting its rung is distinguishable from one silently dropped, and `grep` counts the remaining work.
**Fill order.** PP3.1 creates the row; PP3.2 fills `reproduction PoC`; PP3.3 fills `verdict`; PP4 fills `F-NN` with the ledger row when the fix lands. Severity is the report's post-review figure and PP3.3 may move it, but only with a reproduction or a refutation recorded against the row.
